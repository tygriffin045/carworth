How we pick

We keep a product only if it solves a real car problem and the failure mode is obvious. A dash cam that needs a separate SD card still counts. A jump starter that cannot start the engine size on the box does not. We ignore peak-amp marketing and no-name 8000A claims.

Questions people ask

Do I need a dash cam and a jump starter?

Yes, they solve different problems. The camera is for the crash you did not cause. The jump pack is for the morning the car will not start. Buy the VIOFO and the NOCO before a mount or a radar detector.

Is a 6000A jump starter better than the NOCO?

Peak amps are a marketing number. The GB40 is rated for engines up to 6.0L gas and 3.0L diesel, and it has spark-proof clamps. A no-name 6000A pack is not automatically stronger.

Will WeatherTech fit my car?

Only the listing that names your year, cab, and row. A universal mat is a different product. If the listing does not name your vehicle, do not buy it.
